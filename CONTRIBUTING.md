# Contributing to markpublish

Thank you for your interest in markpublish!

markpublish is open source, but **currently not open to code contributions**.
It is developed and maintained by a single author, and for now I prefer
to keep design and implementation in one hand. For that reason,
**pull requests are currently not accepted** and will be closed without review.
Please don't take this personally, it keeps the project manageable.

## What is welcome

- **Bug reports:** Open an issue with steps to reproduce, your
  markpublish version and OS.
- **Feature suggestions:** Open an issue describing your use case.
  I read every suggestion, but can't promise to implement it.
- **Documentation errors:** Typos or unclear passages can be
  reported as issues as well.

## Forks

The license explicitly allows you to fork and adapt markpublish
for your own needs. Development setup is described in
[DEVELOPMENT.md](DEVELOPMENT.md).
